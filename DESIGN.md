# Design System: Freeclimb

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Proof"**

A restrained, evidence-led system in the spirit of tnwmarketing.com, madebyshape.co.uk, and karolo.com: light blue and neutral tones, whitespace doing the work that gradients and animation would otherwise do elsewhere. The site is the agency's own proof of competence — it must read as substance over style, so nothing decorative earns a place that a result, a case study, or a clear CTA could occupy instead.

This system explicitly rejects the generic AI-template look (cream/sand palettes, gradient text, uppercase eyebrows, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding, identical icon-card grids) and the typical design-agency portfolio (big experimental type, abstract visuals, creative-process storytelling over business outcomes).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Restrained color: neutrals carry the page, light blue appears sparingly as the single accent
- Warm, humanist single-sans typography — approachable, not corporate-stiff
- Motion limited to state changes (hover/focus) — no scroll choreography
- Proof-first layout: results and case studies lead, ornament follows

**The Sparing Accent Rule.** Light blue covers ≤10% of any given screen. Its rarity is what makes it register as a signal (CTA, link, active state) rather than decoration.

## 4. Elevation

Flat by default. Restrained motion energy means depth is conveyed through spacing and neutral-tone layering, not shadows — consistent with the "substance over flourish" references. If a shadow is introduced later, it should signal state (hover/focus) rather than decorate a resting surface.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let light blue stay rare — accent only, never a dominant surface color.
- **Do** lead every section with proof (results, case studies, named outcomes) before any visual flourish.
- **Do** keep motion to hover/focus feedback only; no scroll-driven or orchestrated sequences.
- **Do** cap body copy at 65–75ch and keep the warm sans legible at small sizes.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use cream/sand SaaS-default palettes, gradient text, uppercase tracked eyebrows, or 01/02/03 numbered scaffolding.
- **Don't** default to identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- **Don't** reach for big experimental type, abstract visuals, or creative-process storytelling — this is a lead-gen site, not a portfolio showreel.
- **Don't** add decorative animation or gradients; restraint is the credibility signal, not the absence of effort.
