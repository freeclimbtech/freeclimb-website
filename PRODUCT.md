# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Local service business owners shopping for a web designer — estate agents, trades, professional services, health & wellness businesses. They're comparing a handful of agencies/freelancers, skeptical of generic "we do websites" pitches, and want to see proof that a new site will actually generate enquiries, not just look nice. Likely evaluating on mobile or between other tasks — needs to grasp the offer and see evidence fast.

## Product Purpose

This is Freeclimb's own marketing website — the shopfront for Thomas Darnley's freelance web design practice. Its job is to convert visiting business owners into enquiries for a fixed-price marketing site package (Webflow / Framer / WordPress).

Success = a visitor understands the offer within seconds, sees credible proof it works (results, case studies, testimonials), and takes one clear action (book a call / enquire) — the site practices what it sells: fast-loading, lead-focused, zero filler.

## Brand Personality

Premium, direct, results-driven. Conversational but confident — not corporate-stiff, not creative-agency flashy. Trust built through demonstrated competence and evidence (specific results, case studies, track record), not through visual flourish or creative-process storytelling. "Substance over style" made visible in the site's own design.

References: [tnwmarketing.com](https://www.tnwmarketing.com/) (proof-density — case studies with specific metrics outnumber sales copy, utilitarian layout, no design flourishes signalling substance over style), [madebyshape.co.uk](https://madebyshape.co.uk/) (conversational-confident tone, business substance over portfolio flash, restraint over ornament), [karolo.com](https://karolo.com/) (minimalist, whitespace-led, work speaks louder than flourishes, business-outcome language over creative-process language). Base direction: light blue / neutral tone palette (final palette decided in DESIGN.md).

## Anti-references

- The generic AI-template look: cream/sand SaaS-default palettes, gradient text, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, numbered scaffolding (01/02/03) applied by reflex, identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- The typical design-agency portfolio: big experimental type, abstract/arty visuals, creative-process storytelling, weak or buried CTAs. This is a lead-gen site for a conversion-focused practice, not a showreel — it should read as evidence-led, not flair-led.
- The cheap/DIY local-business site: cluttered layouts, dated stock photography, weak CTAs.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what it sells** — the site itself must be fast, lead-focused, and conversion-first; it's the clearest proof Freeclimb can offer.
2. **Proof over polish** — lead with specific results, case studies, and named outcomes rather than adjectives or creative flourish.
3. **Restraint over ornament** — no gradients, no decorative animation, no flourish that doesn't earn its place; substance signals credibility more than visual flair does.
4. **One clear action per page** — every page drives toward a single enquiry/booking CTA, no competing paths.
5. **Reuse the system** — draw on Freeclimb's existing component/template library (Cluade workspace) rather than building bespoke patterns for its own site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for any animation. Same default as delivered client work — solid default practice, not a compliance mandate.
